# Daily Operations & Warehouse Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## Overview
Airship Express Supply Chain coordinates inbound warehouse supply intake, stock replenishment, inventory reconciliation, purchase order fulfillment, and department issue requests.

---

## Inbound Supply Receiving Workflow (Stage 1)
1. **Intake & Unloading**: Shipments arriving from suppliers are counted and inspected for packaging integrity.
2. **Item Code & PO Verification**: Incoming items are cross-referenced with active Purchase Orders and supplier delivery receipts.
3. **Validation & Quality Check**: 
   - Item specifications, quantities, and condition are verified against purchase order line items.
   - Any damaged, incomplete, or mismatched items are flagged in the Damaged/Missing Supplies module.
4. **Stock-In Completion**: Validated supplies are accepted into warehouse inventory with updated stock levels and automated stock movement logs.

---

## Inventory Management & Storage (Stage 2)
1. **Storage Location Assignment**: Items are assigned to designated warehouse storage racks, bins, and aisle locations.
2. **Stock Level Monitoring**: Real-time tracking of current stock vs. minimum safety stock thresholds.
3. **Low-Stock Alerts**: Automatic notifications and requisition triggers when item quantities dip below minimum stock.
4. **Internal Requests Fulfillment**: Warehouse staff process internal department requests for tools, equipment, packaging materials, and office supplies.

---

## Operating Schedules & Capacity
- **Warehouse Shifts**: 
  - Morning Receiving & Intake: 06:00 AM – 02:00 PM
  - Afternoon Inventory Auditing & Department Issuance: 02:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  - Night Staging & Stock Organization: 10:00 PM – 06:00 AM
- **Peak Intake Hours**: 09:00 AM – 12:00 PM.

---

## Exception Handling Protocols
- **Mismatched Quantities**: Document discrepancy with supplier delivery receipt before updating inventory.
- **Damaged Supplies**: Photograph items, record discrepancy in Damaged/Missing Supplies, notify Procurement for supplier return or credit.
- **Unverified Items**: Place in quarantine holding area until procurement resolves invoice or PO status.
